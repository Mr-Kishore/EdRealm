EdRealm — Trainer Database, Trainer Search & QR Management (MVP)

For the first version of EdRealm’s application, focus on just three modules:

1. Trainer Database — Store and manage trainer profiles, skills, locations, and experience.
2. Trainer Search — Find trainers based on location, subject expertise, and experience.
3. QR Management — Generate QR codes for each college and training batch to collect student feedback.

Exclude availability management, project history, payment tracking, and advanced automation from the MVP.

C. Trainer Profile Actions

Admins should be able to perform the following actions on any trainer profile:

* Edit Profile — Update personal information, contact details, location, and experience.
* Edit Skills — Add, update, or remove subject expertise records.
* Update Trainer Photo — Replace or remove the trainer's profile photo.
* Update Resume — Upload a new resume or remove the existing one.
* Change Trainer Type — Update the trainer's category (Technical, Aptitude, Soft Skills, etc.).
* Activate/Deactivate Profile — Mark a trainer as active or inactive without deleting the record.
* View Change History — See a log of all modifications made to the trainer's profile.

All profile modifications should be recorded in the audit_logs table.

* View Profile
* View Trainer Photo
* View Resume

3. QR Management & Student Feedback

The QR module should allow admins to create feedback QR codes for each college and training batch.

I recommend supporting both:

* College-level QR codes: A general feedback link for a college.
* Batch-level QR codes: A dedicated link for a specific subject, trainer, and batch.

Batch-level QR codes should be the primary option because they allow EdRealm to identify which trainer and training program students are rating.

When an admin disables a QR code, the associated form should stop accepting new submissions.

4. Minimum Database Tables for the MVP

Use PostgreSQL or another relational database.

You can build the MVP with these 9 tables:

No.	Table	Purpose
1	users	Admin accounts and login details
2	trainers	Personal and professional information including photo
3	trainer_skills	Subjects, domains, trainer type, and expertise
4	colleges	College information
5	feedback_qr_codes	QR records and linked college/batch information
6	feedback_questions	Configurable feedback questions
7	feedback_responses	Individual feedback submissions
8	feedback_answers	Individual answers and ratings
9	audit_logs	Records important administrative changes

For location, store the city and state directly in the trainer profile for the MVP. You can normalize locations into separate tables later if necessary.

The feedback_qr_codes table should contain the college, subject, trainer, and batch references needed to associate feedback with the correct training session.

Use a secure, unique token for each QR code instead of exposing database IDs in public URLs.
